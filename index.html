About us

Sport, organised properly

Kickback_.cal brings people together through competitive sport. We design, run and promote tournaments where players get a well-organised event and visitors get a day worth attending.

To date we have hosted tournaments in badminton and pickleball. Those are where we started, not where we stop: our events are open to any sport. Each one involved the full operation: planning the format, coordinating the venue and the day, managing prize money and keeping the competition running on schedule.

Food and beverage stalls are part of every event. They turn a tournament into an occasion, for the players on court and for everyone watching.

We run Kickback_.cal as a self-sustaining operation. Each event is budgeted, resourced and accounted for, so the initiative can fund its own next tournament while keeping the participant experience at the centre.

Our approach is simple: take responsibility for the whole event, work as one team and hold every tournament to the same standard.
